## Map data and tiles

The base map is rendered from OpenFreeMap vector tiles (https://openfreemap.org), which are built from OpenMapTiles
(https://openmaptiles.org) and OpenStreetMap data.

- Data: (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright, licensed under the Open Database

The attribution "OpenFreeMap (c) OpenMapTiles Data from OpenStreetMap" must stay visible on the map.
